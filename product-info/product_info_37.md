## Customer FAQ

**Q: Will the latex gaskets degrade?**

Yes — latex degrades from sun exposure, ozone, and skin oils; expect to replace gaskets every 1-2 seasons of regular use.

**Q: Can I wear this for whitewater?**

Yes — for serious whitewater, pair with a dry suit or splash bib for full lower-body coverage.
