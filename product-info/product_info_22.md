## Customer FAQ

**Q: Can I use this harness for ice climbing?**

Yes — the leg loops are sized to accommodate winter layering, though dedicated ice harnesses may offer thicker padding.

**Q: How do I size the harness?**

Measure your waist at the hipbone level and consult the size chart. The harness should sit snug at the hips without restricting breathing.
