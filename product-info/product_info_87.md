## Customer FAQ

**Q: How do I prevent the tube from freezing?**

The insulated tube delays freezing. For below-freezing weather, blow water back into the bladder after each sip.

**Q: Can I drink electrolyte mix from the bladder?**

Yes, but rinse and dry thoroughly after each use to prevent residue and bacterial growth.
