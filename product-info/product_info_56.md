**Q: Is this saltwater-rated?**

The 3000 size is suitable for light inshore saltwater (small redfish, sea trout). Rinse thoroughly with fresh water after each saltwater use.

**Q: How do I maintain the drag?**

Loosen the drag fully when storing the reel. Replace the carbon fiber washers every 2-3 years of regular use.
