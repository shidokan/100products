## Customer FAQ

**Q: Will these fit my flexible hiking boots?**

Crampons require boots stiff enough to support full-body weight on the front points. Use semi-stiff or B2-rated boots minimum.

**Q: Can I use them on mixed rock and ice?**

Yes — the steel construction handles mixed terrain. For pure rock approaches, remove crampons.
