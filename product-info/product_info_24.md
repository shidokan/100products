**Q: Should I size down for performance?**

Many climbers size down 0.5-1 full size from street shoe size for performance fit; cragsters tend to size to street size.

**Q: Can these be resoled?**

Yes — the rubber sole can be replaced by most cobblers specializing in climbing shoes, typically 1-2 times before the shoe loses its shape.
