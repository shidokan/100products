## Customer FAQ

**Q: Why pealess?**

Traditional whistles use a small ball that can stick when wet, frozen, or fouled. Pealess design has no internal moving parts to fail.

**Q: Can I attach it to a vest?**

Yes — the lanyard hole accepts standard PFD whistle clips or paracord.
