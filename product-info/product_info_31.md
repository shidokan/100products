## Customer FAQ

**Q: Can I use this for whitewater?**

This paddle is designed for flat water and mild current. Whitewater paddles have a shorter length and reinforced blades.

**Q: How do I choose the right length?**

For flat-water paddling, use a length 25-30 cm longer than your height; for narrower boats, shorter is better.
