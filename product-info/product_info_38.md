## Customer FAQ

**Q: Is this suitable for whitewater?**

Whitewater requires a tighter fit and stiffer construction; use a dedicated whitewater spray skirt for class III+ paddling.

**Q: How do I clean the neoprene?**

Rinse with fresh water after each use, drip-dry out of direct sunlight; occasional mild soap for stubborn stains.
