## Customer FAQ

**Q: Will this work for winter camping?**

R-value of 4.5 is suitable for three-season and shoulder-season cold weather. For deep winter (-20°F and below), pair with a closed-cell foam pad.

**Q: How long does it take to inflate?**

Manual valve takes 8-10 breaths to fully inflate. Built-in pump models inflate in 60 seconds.
