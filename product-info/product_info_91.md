## Customer FAQ

**Q: How does this compare to merino?**

Synthetic fleece dries faster, is more durable, and is less expensive than merino. Merino is naturally odor-resistant; fleece is not.

**Q: Can I wear this as an outer layer in summer?**

Mid-weight fleece is too warm for summer use. Choose the lightweight version for warmer conditions.
