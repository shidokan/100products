**Q: How long does solar charging take?**

Solar provides supplemental charge only — full sun for 8 hours adds approximately 30% to the battery. USB-C is the primary charging method.

**Q: Can I leave it on all night?**

Yes — at low brightness it runs for 30 hours; perfect for overnight tent illumination.
