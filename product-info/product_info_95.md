## Customer FAQ

**Q: Are these too warm for summer hiking?**

Merino is naturally temperature-regulating. Mid-weight is comfortable in moderate-to-cold weather; for hot weather, choose the lightweight version.

**Q: Will they shrink?**

Pre-shrunk wool minimizes shrinking, but high heat in dryer can shrink up to 1 size. Tumble dry low or air-dry for longest life.
