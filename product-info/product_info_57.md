## Customer FAQ

**Q: Should I use a fluorocarbon leader?**

Yes — for clear water and line-shy species, a 3-5 ft fluorocarbon leader (8-12 lb) reduces visibility.

**Q: Will it cut into my reel spool?**

Bury 8-10 wraps of monofilament backing first; braid alone can slip on the bare spool.
