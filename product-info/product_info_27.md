## Customer FAQ

**Q: Should I use the wire-gate at the top or bottom?**

Wire-gate at the bolt (top) — reduced gate flutter prevents accidental unclipping during dynamic falls.

**Q: Can I extend these with a sling?**

Yes — the wire-gate can be clipped through any sewn sling for extension, useful for trad placements.
