## Customer FAQ

**Q: Will these fit over my prescription glasses?**

These are not specifically OTG (over-the-glasses); for slim eyewear it may work, but dedicated OTG goggles are recommended for thicker frames.

**Q: How do I prevent fogging?**

Avoid wiping the inside of the double-pane lens. Allow goggles to ventilate when not in use; never store them in your jacket where moisture can build up.
