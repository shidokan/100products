## Customer FAQ

**Q: Will the straps damage trees?**

The included tree-friendly straps are 1.5 in wide and meet Leave No Trace standards for tree protection.

**Q: Do I need an underquilt?**

For temperatures above 60°F, a hammock alone is comfortable. Below that, an underquilt provides bottom-side insulation that a sleeping bag alone cannot.
