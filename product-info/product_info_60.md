## Customer FAQ

**Q: Does this filter viruses?**

No — viruses (e.g., norovirus) are smaller than 0.2 micron. For virus protection, add chemical or UV treatment.

**Q: How do I clean a clogged filter?**

Backflush with clean water; if performance doesn't recover, scrub the ceramic element with the included brush.
