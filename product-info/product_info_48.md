## Customer FAQ

**Q: How does merino compare to synthetics?**

Merino is warmer when wet, naturally odor-resistant, and more comfortable against skin. Synthetics dry faster and are more durable.

**Q: Can I wear this as a single layer for running?**

The midweight is best as a base under additional layers in cold weather. For running, choose a lightweight version.
