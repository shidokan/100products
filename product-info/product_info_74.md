## Customer FAQ

**Q: How do I adjust declination?**

Use a small screwdriver to set the declination value for your area (varies by location). Reset annually as magnetic north shifts.

**Q: Will it work near electronics?**

Keep compass away from electronics, large metal objects, and magnets which interfere with the magnetic needle.
