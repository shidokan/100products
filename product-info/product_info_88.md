## Customer FAQ

**Q: Will it work on cloudy water?**

UV requires reasonably clear water. Pre-filter cloudy water through cloth or a basic filter before UV treatment.

**Q: Is it effective against viruses unlike most pump filters?**

Yes — UV is one of the few field methods that kills viruses (e.g., norovirus, hepatitis A) along with bacteria and protozoa.
