## Customer FAQ

**Q: How do I use it?**

Scrape magnesium shavings into your tinder, then strike the ferrocerium rod with the back of a knife or the included striker to spark.

**Q: Does it work in rain?**

Yes — both magnesium and ferrocerium spark in any weather. Keep your tinder dry separately.
