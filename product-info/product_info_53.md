## Customer FAQ

**Q: Why stockingfoot vs. bootfoot?**

Stockingfoot allows you to choose dedicated wading boots with appropriate sole (felt or studded rubber) for the river substrate.

**Q: How do I prevent leaks?**

Avoid sitting on rough surfaces, store hung up (not folded), and patch small holes immediately with the included repair patch.
