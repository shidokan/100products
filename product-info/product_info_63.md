## Customer FAQ

**Q: Can I bring these on an airplane?**

No — pressurized fuel canisters are prohibited in checked and carry-on baggage. Buy fuel at your destination.

**Q: How do I dispose of empty canisters?**

Use a fuel canister recycling tool (sold separately) to puncture the canister fully empty, then recycle as scrap metal.
