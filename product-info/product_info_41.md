## Customer FAQ

**Q: Are these suitable for icy slopes?**

The stainless steel crampons grip well on packed snow and light ice. For glacier travel or hard ice, mountaineering crampons are required.

**Q: Can I use them with hiking boots?**

Yes — the binding fits most insulated winter boots and many three-season hiking boots.
