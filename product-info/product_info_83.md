## Customer FAQ

**Q: Is this a tent replacement?**

A bivy is a single-person minimal shelter — less protection from weather than a tent but lighter and faster to set up. Best for solo trips with reliable weather forecasts.

**Q: Will I overheat in summer?**

The hooped face opening provides ventilation. For warm weather, leave the face zipper partially open and pair with a lightweight sleeping bag.
