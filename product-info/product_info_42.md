## Customer FAQ

**Q: Is this suitable for ice climbing?**

This is a general-purpose mountaineering axe. For vertical ice climbing, use a technical tool with a reverse-curve pick.

**Q: How do I size the axe?**

Hold the axe by the head with the spike pointing down; the spike should reach approximately your ankle bone for general mountaineering use.
