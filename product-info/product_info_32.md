## Customer FAQ

**Q: Is this approved for whitewater?**

Type III is approved for general boating and calm water. For whitewater, use a Type V high-buoyancy vest.

**Q: How often should I replace the vest?**

Inspect annually; replace if foam shows compression, fabric is faded by UV exposure, or stitching is damaged.
