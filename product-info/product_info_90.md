## Customer FAQ

**Q: How does this compare to iodine?**

Chlorine dioxide tastes better and is effective against Cryptosporidium (which iodine is not). Iodine is contraindicated for pregnant women and people with thyroid issues.

**Q: Can I use these instead of a filter?**

Tablets are excellent backup but slow (30 minutes). For frequent use, a filter or UV purifier is faster.
